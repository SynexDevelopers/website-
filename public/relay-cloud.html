Projects / Relay Cloud

Electrical supply planning across locations.

A company-scoped web application for electrical manufacturers and suppliers. Connect exact components, production requirements, inspected stock and reviewed shortage recovery.

Discuss Relay Cloud

What Relay Cloud includes

  • Private company workspaces and role-based permissions
  • Production orders, BOMs, inventory and supplier quotations
  • Reviewed invoices, payment records and operational reports
  • Authenticator enrollment with locally generated QR codes

Installation and availability

Teams working across locations access a centrally hosted service. Hosting and release acceptance are required before customer rollout.

Built by Synex Developers. Available for a guided demonstration; no client results or uptime guarantees are claimed.
