Projects / Relay Offline

Keep office work moving when internet is unavailable.

A dedicated Ubuntu office server connects several browser or Windows desktop clients to one local PostgreSQL database. Approved staff can work over the office LAN during the signed licence term.

Discuss Relay Offline

What Relay Offline includes

  • Local inventory, production planning, documents and reports
  • Online initial activation and new staff approval
  • Signed licence renewals and seat/location allowances
  • Local sign-in with password and authenticator protection

Installation and availability

The office server, LAN and power must remain available. The Windows pilot installer is unsigned. A customer installation needs certificate enrollment and hardware acceptance; this is not a separate database on every desktop.

Built by Synex Developers. Available for a guided demonstration; no client results or uptime guarantees are claimed.
